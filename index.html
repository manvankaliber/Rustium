      How the numbers are counted
      • All counts use tracked, non-ignored text files. Lockfiles and binary assets are skipped.
      • “Lines of code” counts nonblank lines, including docs and configuration. “Without comment-only lines” removes full-line comments in recognized languages; inline-comment lines stay counted.
      • Actual LOC counts nonblank lines in recognized source extensions after removing recognized comments. Markdown, YAML, TOML, build/config files, ownership files, readmes, lockfiles, ignored files, and unsupported formats are excluded.
      • Language share uses actual LOC. Source size is the byte size of included source files, divided by one billion.
      • Google mentions are case-insensitive whole-word matches in tracked text. Obsolete-function matches are a heuristic for declarations near deprecated, obsolete, or removal markers—not a safe-to-delete list.
      Counts come from tracked, non-ignored files. Comment parsing is language-aware but heuristic. Updated on each push to main.
